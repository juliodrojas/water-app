Household Leak Checklist

Inspect your primary high-consumption fixtures systematically.

  • Add food coloring to the tank. If color enters the bowl before flushing within 15 minutes, the flapper is leaking.
  • Ensure no indoor fixtures or sprinklers run for two hours; check if the meter indicator rotates.
  • Inspect under-sink valves, outdoor hose connections, and aerator seals for slow seepage.
  • Inspect the pressure relief pipe for steady pooling or moisture accumulation.

Water-Saving Tips

Standard showerheads consume up to 2.5 gpm. Upgrading to a certified 1.5 gpm aerating head saves roughly 2,900 gallons per household annually without sacrificing pressure.
Run cycles strictly before 9:00 AM or after 7:00 PM to mitigate midday evaporation loss. Utilize cycle-and-soak run times on compacted slopes to stop runoff.
Washing dishes by hand generally expends 20 gallons compared to modern Energy Star dishwashers consuming under 4 gallons per complete cycle.
